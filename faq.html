Before you go

Considered answers to common questions.

What does The Travel Curators plan?

We design bespoke journeys around your interests, pace, and preferences, from stays and transport to thoughtful local experiences.

How far in advance should I get in touch?

There is no single perfect lead time. Contact us as soon as you have an idea, and we will advise on availability and the best way forward.

Can you plan travel for families or groups?

Yes. We tailor each itinerary to the people travelling, including family-friendly pacing, multi-generational needs, and group interests.

Where are your offices?

Our offices are in Sydney, Australia and Guangzhou, China. We work with a trusted network of local specialists around the world.

How do I begin planning?

Send us an enquiry through our contact page or email support@TTC.net. A curator will be in touch within one working day.
